Column Name: Author
Definition: The name of the person or people responsible for writing or publishing the clinical trial study.

Detailed Extraction Instructions:
- Step 1: Locate the author information, typically found at the beginning of the document, often in the header or title page.
- Step 2: Extract the full names of all listed authors.
- Step 3: If multiple authors are listed, record all names separated by commas or semicolons.
- Step 4: If no author information is explicitly stated, search for a section labeled "Authors," "Contributors," or similar.
- Step 5: If no author information can be found, conclude "not present."

Key Reminders:
- Ensure all author names are extracted completely and accurately.
- Do not include affiliations or other information, only the names of the authors.
- If the author is a group or organization, record the full name of the group or organization.
