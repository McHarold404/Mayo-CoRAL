Column Name: Year
Definition: The year in which the clinical trial was published.

Detailed Extraction Instructions:
- Step 1: Locate the publication date of the clinical trial document. This is typically found near the title, in the header, or in the publication information section.
- Step 2: Identify the year from the publication date. The year should be a four-digit number (e.g., 2023, 2024).
- Step 3: If the publication date is not explicitly stated, look for a copyright date or a date of acceptance. Use the year from these dates if the publication date is unavailable.
- Step 4: If no date information is found, conclude "not present."

Key Reminders:
- Ensure the extracted year is the publication year, not the year the study was conducted or the year the data was collected.
- If multiple dates are present, prioritize the publication date.
- If only a month and year are given, extract the year.
