Column Name: Trial Name
Definition: The title or identifier of the clinical trial being referred to in the study.

Detailed Extraction Instructions:
- Step 1: Search for a title or identifier that is explicitly described as the name of a clinical trial. This may be found in the title, abstract, or introduction of the document.
- Step 2: Look for keywords or phrases such as "clinical trial," "study name," "trial identifier," or "protocol number" that may precede or accompany the trial name.
- Step 3: Extract the full name or identifier of the trial, ensuring that it is not a reference to a different study or publication.
- Step 4: If no trial name or identifier is explicitly stated, mark as "not present."

Key Reminders:
- Be careful not to confuse the trial name with the publication title or other study identifiers.
- Ensure the extracted name is the full and correct name of the clinical trial.
