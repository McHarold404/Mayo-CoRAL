**Task**  
You are an expert in extracting section outlines from clinical trial reports. Your task is to identify the major sections of a clinical trial report that follow the IMRaD structure (Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion) and return them in a specified JSON format with their starting first 5 words.
Each item MUST have exactly three keys:

{
    "title": "<Section Heading>",
    "start": "<Exact first 5 words of the first sentence of the section>",
    "end" : "<Exact last 5 words of the last sentence of the section>",
    "page number": "<Page number of the section>"
}

*** Instructions to follow:
1. Do not include the section title in the 5 words that start the section.
2. The first section is usually the "Abstract" followed by the "Introduction". If you don't find "Abstract", assume that title and give it for the first section. It usually starts just after the title of the paper.
3. Give the title of the section as it appears in the text. 
4. Don't give the acknowledgments or references sections.
5. Use page numbers starting from 1. Not the ones written on the pages as they are likely parts of a journal, hence not starting from 1.

**Example of the required JSON format**
[
  {
    "title": "Introduction",
    "start": "Prostate cancer remains a lethal",
    "end": "the treatment of prostate cancer.",
    "page number": "2"
  },
  {
    "title": "Methods",
    "start": "We conducted a multi-center, phase",
    "end": "the primary endpoint of the study.",
    "page number": "3"
  }
]

Important Instruction: Return only the JSON — no extra prose, markdown, or code fences.
