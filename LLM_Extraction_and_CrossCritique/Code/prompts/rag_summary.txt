**Task**:  
Given the first two pages of a clinical trial publication, generate a concise, structured summary in two paragraphs. The summary should present key trial metadata and compare the treatment and control arms clearly.

**Instructions**:

1. **In Paragraph 1**, include:
   - Full **trial name** and **publication source** (journal, year)
   - **Trial design** (e.g., randomized, open-label, multi-arm multi-stage, etc.)
   - **Trial registration ID** (e.g., ClinicalTrials.gov NCT number)
   - **Study population** (eligibility criteria, number of patients randomized)
   - Clear identification of the **treatment arm** and the **control arm**, specifying:
     - Full treatment composition (drug names, doses, durations)
     - Patient count in each arm
     - Time period of recruitment

2. **In Paragraph 2**, summarize:
   - **Follow-up duration**
   - **Overall survival (OS) hazard ratio**
   - Other key outcomes (e.g., failure-free survival, PFS, MFS, cancer-specific survival)
   - **Adverse event rates** (grade 3–5, by arm)
   - Any notable interpretations (e.g., “no significant OS difference,” “AAP favored in intermediate outcomes”)
   - Include the **names of authors, if there is too many, use the research template "<First Author name> et al.""

**Tone**:  
Professional, neutral, and information-dense. Aim for clarity and completeness without adding interpretation beyond what's in the first two pages.
