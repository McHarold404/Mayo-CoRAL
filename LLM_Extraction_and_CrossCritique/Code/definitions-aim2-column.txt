NCT - National Clinical Trial identifier, a unique identification number assigned to a clinical trial.

PMID - A unique identifier assigned to a clinical trial publication in the PubMed database.

Authors - The name of the person or people responsible for writing or publishing the clinical trial study.

Year - The year in which the clinical trial was published.

Original/Follow Up - The designation of the clinical trial as either an original study or a follow-up study.

Study name - Name of the clinical study or experiment being referenced.

Trial phase - The stage or step in the clinical trial process, typically denoted by a Roman numeral (e.g. I, II, III, IV), which defines the scope, objectives, and duration of the trial.

Number of arms - The total count of experimental treatment arms and control arms included in the clinical trial.

Original publication or Follow-up - The designation of the publication as either an original study or a follow-up study.

Cancer type – The specific disease/indication under study (e.g., metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer, NSCLC, melanoma), including stage/setting when relevant.

Treatment regimen – The full, prespecified therapy given to the experimental arm(s): drug names, doses, schedules, cycles, and any radiation/surgery/ADT details.

Name of ICI – The exact immune checkpoint inhibitor used (e.g., pembrolizumab, nivolumab, atezolizumab, ipilimumab).

Class of ICI – The target pathway of the ICI (e.g., PD-1 inhibitor, PD-L1 inhibitor, CTLA-4 inhibitor, LAG-3 inhibitor, TIGIT inhibitor).

Monotherapy/combination – Whether the ICI is given alone (monotherapy) or with other anticancer treatments (combination).

Type of combination – If combination, specify the partner(s): e.g., ICI + chemotherapy, ICI + targeted therapy, ICI + hormone therapy, ICI + another ICI (dual ICI), ICI + radiotherapy, or triplet/multimodal combos.

Control regimen – The comparator treatment assigned to the control group (e.g., placebo, best supportive care, standard of care chemo X, ADT alone, physician’s choice).

Type of control – Categorize the control as placebo-controlled, active-controlled (specific drug/therapy), standard-of-care, best supportive care, or observation.
Total sample size – Total number of participants randomized/enrolled for analysis across all arms.

Lines of treatment – How many prior systemic therapy “lines” a patient has received for the disease setting being studied (e.g., 1L = first-line, 2L = after one prior line); also used to specify the line under investigation.

Clinical setting in relation to surgery – Where the therapy sits on the surgical timeline:
	Neoadjuvant (before surgery),
	Adjuvant (after surgery),
	Perioperative (pre- and post-op plan),
	Maintenance (post–initial therapy to maintain response),
	Metastatic/recurrent (unresectable) (no curative surgery planned).

Is PD-L1 positivity inclusion criteria – Whether PD-L1 expression above a prespecified cutoff (e.g., CPS ≥10, TPS ≥50%) was required to enroll (Yes/No).

Is any other biomarker used for inclusion – Whether eligibility required another biomarker (e.g., MSI-H/dMMR, TMB-high, EGFR/ALK status, BRCA, PD-1/LAG-3 co-expression), with the specific marker and cutoff recorded if Yes.

Primary endpoint – The prespecified main outcome the trial is powered to test (e.g., OS, PFS, pCR, EFS, RFS/DFS, ORR).

Primary multiple, composite, or co-primary endpoints? – Indicates if the study used:
	Co-primary endpoints (≥2 primaries tested),
	A composite endpoint (combined event definition), or
	A multiple-primary framework (more than one primary family of endpoints).

Secondary endpoint – Additional prespecified outcomes (e.g., DoR, time to response, CBR, QoL, safety, landmark survival rates, biomarker-defined efficacy).

Type of follow-up given – What follow-up data are reported (e.g., median follow-up, range/IQR, landmark timepoints, data-cut date, minimum follow-up), and whether survival is censored per KM methods.

Follow-up duration for primary endpoint(s) in months – The amount of follow-up available at the time of the primary analysis, reported in months:
	[Overall] – Median follow-up across the entire study population.
	[Rx] – Median follow-up among participants in the treatment/experimental arm.
	[Control] – Median follow-up among participants in the control arm.
