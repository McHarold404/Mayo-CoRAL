months). Relevance
These results indicate that orteronel is likely not an effective androgen axis inhibitor compared with recently approved agents
in this setting. Higher than anticipated OS of patients in the control arm is reﬂective of therapeutic advancements made
over the past decade in men with metastatic prostate cancer.
