androgen synthesis (ie, CYP17 [CYP17 hydroxylase,

glands.1-5 More recently, two direct, potent, and speciﬁc

enzalutamide, were approved on the basis of improved
survival in phase III trials.1,6,7 Currently, the most
common sequencing of these agents in the treatment of
metastatic prostate cancer is abiraterone acetate fol-
lowed by enzalutamide or docetaxel.8 One challenge
with abiraterone acetate is the requirement for coad-
ministration of corticosteroids because of inhibition of
CYP17 hydroxylase.1 This results in diminished cortisol
production and leads to secondary mineralocorticoid

ties, hypertension, and edema.1
