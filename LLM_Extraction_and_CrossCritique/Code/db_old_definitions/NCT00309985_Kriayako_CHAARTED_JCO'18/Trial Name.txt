Extensive Disease in Prostate Cancer) trial with more mature follow-up and focus on tumor volume. Patients and Methods
In this phase III study, 790 patients with metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer were equally
randomly assigned to receive either ADT in combination with docetaxel 75 mg/m2 for up to six cycles
