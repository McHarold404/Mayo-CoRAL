Abstract
Background: Docetaxel (D) at the time of starting androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) for metastatic castrate
naive prostate cancer shows a clear survival benefit for patients with high-volume (HV) disease. It is unclear
whether patients with low-volume (LV) disease benefit from early D.
Objective: To deﬁne the overall survival (OS) of aggregate data of patient subgroups from the CHAARTED and
GETUG-AFU15 studies, deﬁned by metastatic burden (HV and LV) and time of metastasis occurrence (at
diagnosis or after prior local treatment [PRLT]).
