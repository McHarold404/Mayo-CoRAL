The GETUG-AFU15 trial is a French multicenter open-label randomized
study [13]. From October 2004 to December 2008, 385 patients were
enrolled including 193 in the ADT arm and 192 in the ADT plus D arm. Eligible patients were randomized in a 1:1 ratio to ADT plus D (75 mg/m2
for 21 d, up to nine cycles) or ADT alone. ADT consisted of orchiectomy or
luteinizing hormone-releasing hormone agonists, alone or combined
