deﬁnition, biochemical progression was deﬁned as a previous conﬁrmed

with a minimum increase of 5 ng/ml. For patients without a previous PSA
decrease of 50%, progression was deﬁned as a PSA increase at least 25%
above the nadir and at least 5 ng/ml [17]. In patients with measurable
lesions, radiographic progression was deﬁned using Response Evaluation

lesions only, radiographic progression was deﬁned as one or more new
