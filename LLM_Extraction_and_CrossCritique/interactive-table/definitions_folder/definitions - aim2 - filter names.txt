ICI Class — The checkpoint pathway targeted by the drug (e.g., PD-1, PD-L1, CTLA-4).

ICI Name — The specific immune-checkpoint inhibitor (e.g., pembrolizumab, nivolumab, atezolizumab, ipilimumab).

Cancer Type — The tumor indication/histology being studied (e.g., NSCLC, melanoma, HCC).

Type of Therapy — Whether the ICI is used alone (monotherapy) or with other agents (combination).

Type of combination (Treatment Arm) — For combination trials, what the ICI is paired with in the experimental arm (e.g., chemotherapy, targeted therapy, radiation, anti-angiogenic, another ICI).

Control Arm — The comparator regimen given to the control group (e.g., chemotherapy, targeted therapy, placebo/observation, another ICI).

Clinical Setting — The disease stage/line of therapy at treatment (e.g., neoadjuvant, adjuvant, first-line metastatic, second-line+, maintenance/consolidation).

Trial Phase — Stage of clinical development (here typically Phase 2 for exploratory efficacy or Phase 3 for confirmatory, standard-of-care comparisons).

Type of Study — Study design category (e.g., randomized controlled vs single-arm; double-blind vs open-label, etc.).

Primary Endpoint — The main outcome the trial is powered to test (e.g., OS, PFS, ORR, DFS/EFS, pCR).

Included in MA — Whether the study is included in the meta-analysis dataset (MA = meta-analysis).
