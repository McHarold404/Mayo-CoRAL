# ICI Class

* **All** – show every class.
* **PD-1** – receptor “brake” on T cells; blocking re-activates T cells.
* **PD-L1** – ligand/key on tumor/immune cells; blocking prevents PD-1 shutdown.
* **CTLA-4** – T-cell receptor competing with CD28; blocking boosts priming in lymph nodes.

# ICI Name (drug → class | mechanism | common uses)

* **Atezolizumab** → PD-L1 | blocks PD-L1 | NSCLC, TNBC, urothelial.
* **Avelumab** → PD-L1 | blocks PD-L1; ADCC | Merkel cell, urothelial, RCC.
* **Cemiplimab** → PD-1 | blocks PD-1 | cSCC, BCC, NSCLC.
* **Durvalumab** → PD-L1 | blocks PD-L1 | Stage III NSCLC (post-CRT), ES-SCLC.
* **Ipilimumab** → CTLA-4 | blocks CTLA-4 | melanoma, RCC (often w/ nivolumab).
* **Nivolumab** → PD-1 | blocks PD-1 | melanoma, NSCLC, RCC, Hodgkin, others.
* **Pembrolizumab** → PD-1 | blocks PD-1 | melanoma, NSCLC, HNSCC, MSI-H tumors.
* **Tremelimumab** → CTLA-4 | blocks CTLA-4 | HCC; combos in solid tumors.

# Cancer Type (key ICIs | target/mechanism | notes/examples)

* **Bladder/Urothelial**: Atezolizumab, Pembrolizumab, Nivolumab, Avelumab | PD-1/PD-L1 | Advanced/metastatic.
* **Breast (TNBC)**: Atezolizumab+nab-paclitaxel, Pembrolizumab | PD-1/PD-L1 | Mainly PD-L1+.
* **Colorectal**: Pembrolizumab, Nivolumab | PD-1 | For MSI-H/dMMR only.
* **Esophageal/GEJ**: Nivolumab, Pembrolizumab | PD-1 | Metastatic/adjuvant.
* **Gastric/GEJ**: Pembrolizumab, Nivolumab | PD-1 | PD-L1+ advanced disease.
* **HNSCC**: Pembrolizumab, Nivolumab | PD-1 | Recurrent/metastatic.
* **HCC**: Atezolizumab+Bevacizumab, Durvalumab, Tremelimumab | PD-L1/CTLA-4 | 1L standard changed (e.g., IMbrave150).
* **Melanoma**: Nivolumab, Pembrolizumab, Ipilimumab, Tremelimumab | PD-1/CTLA-4 | Large benefits; combos common.
* **Mesothelioma**: Nivolumab+Ipilimumab | PD-1+CTLA-4 | CheckMate 743.
* **Multiple Myeloma**: ICIs not standard | research only | CAR-T/bispecifics preferred.
* **NSCLC**: Pembrolizumab, Nivolumab, Atezolizumab, Durvalumab, Cemiplimab | PD-1/PD-L1 | SOC for PD-L1+ or metastatic; durvalumab consolidation in stage III.
* **Pancreatic**: Limited | MSI-H/dMMR only | Immune-“cold.”
* **Prostate**: Minimal | PD-1 in MSI-H/dMMR or TMB-high only.
* **RCC**: Nivolumab, Pembrolizumab, Ipilimumab (combos) | PD-1 ± CTLA-4 | SOC in 1L advanced RCC.
* **SCLC**: Atezolizumab, Durvalumab (with chemo) | PD-L1 | Extensive-stage.

# Type of Therapy

* **All** – monotherapy + combinations.
* **Monotherapy** – ICI alone (e.g., pembro in MSI-H CRC; nivo 2L NSCLC; PD-L1-high tumors).
* **Combination therapy** – ICI + other agent(s) to raise responses/broaden activity.

# Type of Combination (Treatment Arm)

* **All** – any regimen.
* **ICI + ICI** – e.g., Nivo+Ipi (melanoma, RCC, mesothelioma); synergistic checkpoints.
* **ICI + Chemo** – e.g., Pembro + platinum (NSCLC); cytoreduction ↑ immunogenicity.
* **ICI + TKI** – e.g., Pembro + Lenvatinib (RCC, endometrial); VEGF/oncogenic pathway effects.
* **ICI + MEKi** – investigational (melanoma); sensitizes to immune attack.
* **ICI + Anti-VEGF** – e.g., Atezolizumab + Bevacizumab (HCC); normalize vessels, ↑ infiltration.
* **ICI + Radiation** – e.g., Pembro + SBRT; potential abscopal effect.
* **ICI + Vaccine** – experimental; vaccine primes, ICI sustains.
* **ICI + BRAFi + MEKi** – BRAF-mutant melanoma triple (Dabrafenib + Trametinib + Pembro).
* **ICI + Chemo + Anti-VEGF** – triplet in NSCLC (Atezo + Bev + Chemo).
* **ICI + ICI + Chemo** – e.g., Nivo + Ipi + Chemo (CheckMate 9LA).

# Control Arm (comparators)

* **All**.
* **Chemo**; **TKI**; **Interferon**; **Multikinase inhibitor** (e.g., sorafenib in HCC); **mTOR inhibitor** (everolimus in RCC); **Radiation**; **Vaccine**; **Placebo**; **Chemo/Anti-EGFR** (e.g., FOLFOX + cetuximab); **Chemo/Anti-VEGF**; **Chemo/TKI**; **BRAFi + MEKi**; **Best Supportive Care**.

# Clinical Setting

* **All**.
* **Adjuvant** – post-surgery to cut recurrence (e.g., nivo/pembro in melanoma; nivo in NSCLC).
* **Maintenance** – after initial response to maintain control (e.g., durvalumab post-CRT in stage III NSCLC—PACIFIC).
* **Metastatic/Recurrent (Unresectable)** – e.g., pembro 1L NSCLC; nivo+ipi in melanoma.
* **Neoadjuvant** – pre-surgery to shrink/prime (e.g., CheckMate 816 in NSCLC).

# Trial Phase

* **All**.
* **Phase 2** – signal-seeking; ORR/PFS/DoR; tens–hundreds of pts.
* **Phase 3** – confirmatory vs control; OS/PFS/QoL; practice-changing.

# Type of Study

* **All**.
* **Original publication** – first primary-analysis paper (e.g., NEJM/JCO).
* **Follow-up** – longer OS/PFS, safety, subgroup/biomarker, secondary/post-hoc.

# Primary Endpoint (definitions)

* **ORR** – % CR/PR by RECIST (or relevant).
* **OS** – time to death any cause.
* **PFS** – time to progression or death.
* **RFS** – time post-curative therapy to recurrence/death (≈ DFS).
* **Path CR (pCR)** – no viable tumor in resection (± nodes) after neoadjuvant.
* **EFS** – time to prespecified event (e.g., progression precluding surgery, recurrence, second cancer, or death).
* **Safety** – primary focus on AEs (e.g., grade ≥3, SAEs, discontinuations).

# Included in Meta-analysis (MA)

* **All** – regardless of inclusion.
* **Yes** – included in pooled dataset (eligible, extractable endpoints).
* **No** – excluded (design/setting mismatch, insufficient data, duplicate/out of scope).
